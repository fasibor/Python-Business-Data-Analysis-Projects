
# Python Business Data Analysis Projects

A collection of practical Python exercises focused on solving real-world business problems through data analysis.

This repository documents my journey of developing Python skills by working through business scenarios involving sales, supplier performance, inventory management, customer transactions, and procurement analysis.

Rather than focusing only on Python syntax, these exercises demonstrate how programming concepts can be applied to extract insights, identify trends, evaluate performance, and support business decisions.

## Project Overview

The repository covers five major business analysis areas:

### 1. Sales Analysis
Analyzed sales records to understand revenue performance across different regions and months.

**Key Tasks:**
- Identify unique sales regions.
- Calculate total sales by region and month.
- Identify the highest-performing sales month.
- Calculate each region's contribution to total revenue.
- Filter sales records above ₦1.3 million.
- Calculate month-on-month sales growth.
- Rank regions based on sales performance.

### 2. Supplier Performance Analysis
Evaluated supplier reliability and procurement performance using delivery and cost data.

**Key Tasks:**
- Calculate supplier late delivery rates.
- Identify suppliers with late delivery rates above 20%.
- Calculate average procurement cost per shipment.
- Rank suppliers based on delivery performance.
- Identify suppliers with at least 20 shipments and late delivery rates below 10%.
- Calculate the overall late delivery rate.
- Identify suppliers requiring performance improvement.

### 3. Inventory Management Analysis
Examined inventory levels to identify stock shortages and replenishment priorities.

**Key Tasks:**
- Identify products below their reorder levels.
- Calculate replenishment quantities.
- Estimate replenishment costs.
- Calculate the total replenishment budget.
- Rank products by replenishment cost.
- Identify the three most expensive items to replenish.
- Prioritize products with stock levels below 50% of their reorder levels.

### 4. Customer Transaction Analysis
Analyzed customer transactions to understand purchasing behaviour and revenue contribution.

**Key Tasks:**
- Calculate total spending per customer.
- Identify the highest-spending customer.
- Calculate average transaction value.
- Count transactions per customer.
- Identify customers with spending above ₦50,000.
- Calculate customer contributions to total revenue.
- Identify customers who purchased across multiple months.

### 5. Procurement Data Analysis
Cleaned and analyzed procurement records to evaluate purchasing activities, supplier spending, and delivery performance.

**Key Tasks:**
- Identify missing and inconsistent values.
- Standardize supplier and material names.
- Separate procurement status information.
- Calculate purchase order values.
- Calculate total spending on delivered purchase orders.
- Analyze supplier and material spending.
- Identify the highest-spending material category.
- Calculate supplier spending contributions.
- Calculate average delivery delays.
- Identify suppliers with average delays above two days.
- Calculate the percentage of delivered purchase orders.
- Identify materials with the highest cumulative delivery delays.
- Rank suppliers based on delivery performance.
